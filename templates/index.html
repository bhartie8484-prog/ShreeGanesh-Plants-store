Plant Care Tips

Expert advice for your green friends

Sunlight

Most indoor plants need bright, indirect light. Avoid direct harsh sunlight.

Watering

Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Don't overwater your plants.

Temperature

Keep plants in 18-24°C temperature range for optimal growth.

Fertilizer

Feed your plants with organic fertilizer every 2-3 months.
