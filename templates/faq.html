Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about plants, delivery, payments, and returns.

Are online payments real on this website?

No. This project uses a clearly marked demo payment gateway. It never charges a card, bank account, or UPI ID.

How are live plants packed?

Pots and soil are secured before plants are placed in ventilated protective packaging.

How long does delivery take?

Orders usually arrive 3–7 business days after dispatch, depending on the destination.

What if a plant arrives damaged?

Contact us within 24 hours with clear photos and an unboxing video so our team can review the issue.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact support as soon as possible. Changes are possible only before dispatch.
